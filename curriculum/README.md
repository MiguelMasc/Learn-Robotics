# Robotics Curriculum Guide

This curriculum is designed to guide you through the essential topics and skills needed to become proficient in robotics. It covers a wide range of subjects, from basic programming to advanced robotics concepts.

### Foundations (Year 1)
 
| # | Course | Key Topics | Best Resources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Calculus I & II** | Limits, derivatives, integrals, series | MIT OCW 18.01/18.02 (free); Stewart Calculus; Professor Leonard (YouTube, free) |
| 2 | **Intro Programming (Python)** | Data types, control flow, OOP, algorithms | CMU 15-112 (free online); CS50P Harvard (edX, free); *Automate the Boring Stuff* (free) |
| 3 | **Physics I — Mechanics** | Kinematics, Newton's laws, energy, momentum | MIT OCW 8.01 Walter Lewin lectures (free); Halliday/Resnick textbook |
| 4 | **Linear Algebra** | Vectors, matrices, eigenvalues, SVD | MIT OCW 18.06 Gilbert Strang (free — best course available); 3Blue1Brown (YouTube, free) |
| 5 | **Intro to Robotics** | Overview of sensing, acting, planning | CMU 16-311 (free notes); edX Delft Intro to Robotics (free) |

### Core Engineering (Year 2)
 
| # | Course | Key Topics | Best Resources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Differential Equations** | ODEs, Laplace transforms, systems of ODEs | MIT OCW 18.03 (free); Paul's Online Math Notes (free) |
| 2 | **Data Structures & Algorithms** | Graphs, trees, sorting, complexity | MIT OCW 6.006 (free); Coursera Algorithms Princeton (free audit); CLRS textbook |
| 3 | **Physics II — E&M** | Circuits, fields, Faraday's law | MIT OCW 8.02 (free); Walter Lewin lectures (YouTube) |
| 4 | **Probability & Statistics** | Distributions, Bayesian inference | MIT OCW 6.041 (free); *Think Stats* (free PDF); Coursera Probability UCSD |
| 5 | **Circuits & Electronics** | Resistors, op-amps, microcontrollers | MIT OCW 6.002 (free); *Make: Electronics* (Platt); Ben Eater YouTube (free) |
 
### Robotics Core (Year 3)
 
| # | Course | Key Topics | Best Resources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Robot Kinematics & Dynamics** | DH parameters, Jacobians, forward/inverse kinematics | *Modern Robotics* Lynch & Park (free PDF + free Coursera specialization) |
| 2 | **Control Systems** | PID, root locus, Bode, state-space | MIT OCW 6.302 (free); Brian Douglas YouTube (free, ~50 videos); Ogata textbook |
| 3 | **Sensors & Actuators** | LiDAR, IMU, encoders, motor drives | CMU 16-311 notes (free); NVIDIA Jetson tutorials (free) |
| 4 | **Embedded Systems** | ARM Cortex, FreeRTOS, UART/SPI/I2C | *Making Embedded Systems* (White); STM32 ControllersTech (YouTube, free) |
| 5 | **ROS 2 Fundamentals** | Nodes, topics, services, tf2, Gazebo | ROS2 official docs (free); The Construct ROS2 courses |
 
### Specialization & Capstone (Year 4)
 
| # | Course | Key Topics | Best Resources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Computer Vision & Perception** | Feature detection, deep learning, 3D vision | CS231n Stanford (free); *Computer Vision* Szeliski (free PDF); OpenCV docs |
| 2 | **Motion Planning** | A*, RRT, PRM, trajectory optimization | *Planning Algorithms* LaValle (free PDF at planning.cs.uiuc.edu); CMU 16-350 notes |
| 3 | **Machine Learning for Robotics** | CNNs, RL, sim-to-real transfer | fast.ai (free); *Deep Learning* Goodfellow (free at deeplearningbook.org) |
| 4 | **Capstone Design Project** | Full robot system: perception → planning → control | ROS2 + Gazebo + real hardware; GitHub portfolio |
| 5 | **Ethics & Safety** | ISO 10218, ISO/TS 15066, AI ethics | IEEE Ethically Aligned Design (free PDF); Helsinki AI Ethics MOOC (free) |
